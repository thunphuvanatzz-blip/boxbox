Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

BOX BOX uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the BOX BOX | F1 Moments channels to upload our own short videos to our own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram on a schedule. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the connected account (open ID, display name, username) and the permission to upload videos to that account. The app uses the user.info.basic, video.upload and, if enabled, video.publish scopes.
  • YouTube: the connected channel's ID and name, and permission to upload videos and read their status.
  • Facebook and Instagram: the connected Page and its linked Instagram professional account, and permission to publish Reels to them.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform so the app can act on the connected accounts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule our own videos and to check whether each upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and the app does not read anyone else's content or data.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user. The app has no server and no database, and sends data only to the platform APIs it uploads to (TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data goes only to the platform that a video is being uploaded to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be removed at any time by deleting the app's local token files, or from each platform's settings:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings → Business integrations

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
